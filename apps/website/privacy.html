Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Summary

Typing Helper does not collect, store or sell personal data. There are no accounts and no analytics.

What runs on the pages you visit

A content script reads the typing passage on the current page and sends keystrokes to it, only when you press Start or the keyboard shortcut. Page content never leaves your browser.

Network requests

The extension periodically fetches public site rules (CSS selectors for typing sites) from api.typing.njmtech.co.za. The request contains nothing about you or the pages you visit. Cloudflare, which hosts the API, may keep standard request logs (IP address, user agent) for operational purposes.

Stored settings

Your speed, accuracy and key-event mode, and the cached site rules, are kept in the browser's extension storage on your device.

Permissions

  • storage — save your settings and cached site rules.
  • activeTab — talk to the tab you start typing on.
  • alarms — refresh site rules every few hours.
  • debugger (optional, Chromium) — only requested for Trusted mode, used solely to send key presses to the current tab.
